# PA/SA LDCE Paper I: collected papers and keys (gaps only)

Only things on the "missing" list are kept: papers and figures for 2020-2023, official keys the app lacks, and full papers for the circles listed for 2024-2026. Files the app already has were deleted.

## Figure questions (priority A)

- `figure-questions/q<number>.png`: one image per figure question (question text, figure and all four options), cut from VV Academy's retyped papers. There are 709 crops from 81 papers covering 2020-2023, using VV's question numbering. Booklet series (A/B/C/D) can order questions differently, so match on the question text, not only the number.
- `figure-pages/` (2023 scans): 150 dpi renders of the scanned pages that hold figure questions, named by PDF page and question numbers. Pen ticks on the scans are the candidate's own marks.

## Sources and caveats

- **YouTube:** from this cloud machine every watch page hits Google's "unusual traffic" block, so nothing here comes from video frames. Everything came from public web pages instead; each folder's `source.txt` gives the exact links.
- **postalstudy.in** (site of the "Postal Study" channel) and **potoolsblog.in**: public Google Drive scans of the real papers and keys.
- **VV Academy** (`question-paper-vv-retyped/`): free retyped papers with the figures as embedded images. They are not the original scans. The VV files for 2023 UP and 2023 Jharkhand also contained the West Bengal and Karnataka papers, which are split into their own folders.
- **postalwala.com** (`question-paper+key-postalwala/`, 2025 only): free compilations of paper plus answers. The answers are not official.
- Key folders: `answer-key-official` means the source said it is official; `answer-key` means the source didn't say; `answer-key-unofficial` means unofficial.
